## How to Find People to Interview

- DM 5-10 founders/CTOs on LinkedIn. Message: "Hey, I'm building a free AI spend audit tool for startups. Would love 10 mins of your time to understand how you manage AI tool costs. No sales pitch, just research."
- Ask in your college/bootcamp network — anyone working at a startup using AI tools
- Post in r/startups or r/SaaS: "Looking for 10-min interviews about AI tool spending at startups"
- Check IndieHackers — many solo founders actively share their tool stacks
