## FAQ

**Q1: Is this actually free?**
Yes, completely free. No credit card, no login required. We make money when you purchase discounted AI credits through Credex — the audit tool itself will always be free.

**Q2: Do you store my data?**
Only if you choose to save your report by entering your email. Without email, nothing is stored after you close the tab. Form drafts are saved in your browser's localStorage only.

**Q3: How accurate are the recommendations?**
All pricing is verified against official vendor pricing pages as of May 2026. Every source URL is listed in our public PRICING_DATA.md file. We update prices weekly.

**Q4: What AI tools do you cover?**
Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), OpenAI API, Anthropic API, Google Gemini, and Windsurf. We're adding more tools each week.

**Q5: What happens after I enter my email?**
You get a copy of your audit report sent to your inbox. If your estimated savings exceed $200/month, a Credex advisor may reach out to discuss discounted AI credits for your team. You can opt out anytime.

**Q6: Can I share my audit results?**
Yes. Each audit generates a unique shareable URL. Share it with your team or your finance lead to make the case for switching plans.
